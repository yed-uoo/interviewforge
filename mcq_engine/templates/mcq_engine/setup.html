How It Works

  • 1
    Single Page Assessment

    All questions are presented on one page. Review and change answers freely before submitting.

  • 2
    Experience-Aware Difficulty

    Questions are balanced to your level — Fresher (80% Easy), Junior (60% Easy), Mid-Level (50% Easy), Senior (40% Easy).

  • 3
    I Don't Know (Option X)

    Answer honestly. Responses are tracked to support future AI performance breakdowns.

⚠

Before You Begin

Please read these instructions carefully before starting your assessment.

  • Attempt only the questions you genuinely know.
  • If you do not know the answer, select "I Don't Know (Option X)".
  • Do NOT randomly guess unknown questions. Honest responses help the AI accurately identify your strengths and weaknesses.
  • Do NOT use books, websites, ChatGPT, AI tools, friends, or any external resources.
  • This assessment is designed to personalize your learning path, not to maximize your score.
  • After submission, you will receive AI-powered performance analysis and personalized revision recommendations.
