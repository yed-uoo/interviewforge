How It Works

  • 1
    Single Page Assessment

    All questions are presented on one page. Review and change answers freely before submitting.

  • 2
    Balanced Difficulty Budget

    Questions are balanced dynamically: roughly 40% Easy, 30% Medium, and 30% Hard.

  • 3
    I Don't Know (Option X)

    Skip questions honestly. Responses are tracked to support future AI performance breakdowns.

Ready to Begin?

Please read the following before starting:

  • Attempt only the questions you genuinely know.
  • If you do not know the answer, select "I Don't Know (Option X)".
  • Do NOT randomly guess answers.
  • Do NOT copy from books, websites, ChatGPT, or any external resources.
  • This assessment is designed to identify your strengths and weaknesses.
  • Honest answers allow the AI to generate personalized study recommendations.
  • Once the assessment starts, answer all questions before submitting.
